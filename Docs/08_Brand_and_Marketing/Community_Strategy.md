# Community Strategy
PanjoCoin (PNJC)

Version: 1.0
Date: July 2026
Organization: CryptoTengo

# 1. Overview

This document defines the community strategy for the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

The purpose of this strategy is to build a welcoming, informed, and engaged global community that contributes to the long-term development of the ecosystem through collaboration, education, and constructive participation.

Community growth is intended to be organic, transparent, and sustainable.

# 2. Strategic Objectives

The PNJC community strategy aims to:

Build a global community around the ecosystem
Promote blockchain education
Encourage responsible participation
Support open collaboration
Increase developer engagement
Foster constructive discussions
Strengthen long-term ecosystem sustainability
# 3. Community Vision

PNJC seeks to create a community where people can:

Learn about blockchain technology
Participate in ecosystem discussions
Contribute ideas and improvements
Collaborate on open-source initiatives
Support social impact programs
Share knowledge with others

The community is intended to be inclusive and accessible to participants with different levels of technical experience.

# 4. Community Values

The community is guided by the following values:

Transparency
Open communication
Honest project updates
Public documentation
Respect

Community members are expected to:

Treat others respectfully
Welcome diverse viewpoints
Avoid harassment and discrimination
Collaboration

The project encourages:

Knowledge sharing
Constructive feedback
Open-source contributions
Teamwork
Responsibility

Participants should:

Verify information before sharing
Respect community rules
Help maintain a positive environment

# 14. Moderation Principles

Moderation aims to:

Maintain respectful discussions
Protect community members
Reduce spam
Prevent abusive behavior
Encourage constructive dialogue

Moderation decisions should be applied consistently and transparently.

# 15. Code of Conduct

Community participants are expected to:

Be respectful
Act professionally
Avoid personal attacks
Respect privacy
Follow applicable laws
Support an inclusive environment

Repeated violations may result in moderation actions in accordance with community policies.

Growth should be measured by participation and contribution rather than community size alone.

# 20. Summary

The PNJC Community Strategy establishes a framework for building a collaborative, transparent, and globally accessible community.

Its long-term objectives are to:

Promote blockchain education
Encourage meaningful participation
Support open-source collaboration
Foster social impact initiatives
Strengthen the PanjoCoin ecosystem through active community engagement

The strategy emphasizes trust, openness, and responsible growth as the foundation of a sustainable Web3 community.
