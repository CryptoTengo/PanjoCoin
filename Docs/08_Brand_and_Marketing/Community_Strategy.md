## 1. Overview

This document defines the **community strategy** for the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

The purpose of this strategy is to build a welcoming, informed, and engaged global community that contributes to the long-term development of the ecosystem through collaboration, education, and constructive participation.

Community growth is intended to be organic, transparent, and sustainable.

### 1.1. Context

PanjoCoin is a Web3 ecosystem combining blockchain technology, SocialFi, GameFi, charitable initiatives, and transparent governance. The project is built on **Polygon PoS** with a fixed-supply, ownerless utility token.

| **Token Name** | PanjoCoin |

| **Transfer Tax** | 0% |
| **Owner** | None (ownerless) |

## 3. Community Vision

PNJC seeks to create a community where people can:

- Learn about blockchain technology;
- Participate in ecosystem discussions;
- Contribute ideas and improvements;
- Collaborate on open-source initiatives;
- Support social impact programs (Charity Reserve, ClownCare Bridge);
- Share knowledge with others;
- Participate in governance (DAO).

The community is intended to be inclusive and accessible to participants with different levels of technical experience.

- Constructive feedback;

### Responsibility

Participants should:

- ClownCare Bridge (Q2 2027).

## 17. Risk Management

Potential risks include:

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Misinformation** | False or misleading information. | Official channels, education, moderation. |
| **Spam** | Irrelevant or malicious content. | Active moderation, filters. |
| **Fraud Attempts** | Scams and impersonation. | Official verification, warnings. |
| **Impersonation** | Fake accounts and websites. | Official channel verification. |
| **Community Conflicts** | Disagreements and disputes. | Moderation, Code of Conduct. |
| **Inactive Participation** | Low engagement. | Regular events, incentives. |

Mitigation measures include:
- Active moderation;
- Community guidelines (Code of Conduct);
- Official verification channels;
- Educational resources;
- Transparent communication.
