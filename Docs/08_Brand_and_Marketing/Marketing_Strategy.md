## 1. Overview

This document defines the **long-term marketing strategy** for the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

The objective of the strategy is to build a trusted global Web3 brand by promoting education, transparency, community participation, and responsible communication.

Marketing activities are intended to increase awareness of the ecosystem and its technology rather than encourage speculative trading or investment decisions.

### 1.1. Context

PanjoCoin is a Web3 ecosystem combining blockchain technology, SocialFi, GameFi, charitable initiatives, and transparent governance. The project is built on **Polygon PoS** with a fixed-supply, ownerless utility token.

## 18. Risk Management

Potential marketing risks include:

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Misleading Communications** | Unrealistic claims or promises. | Editorial review, disclaimers. |
| **Regulatory Changes** | New marketing regulations. | Compliance review, legal monitoring. |
| **Brand Inconsistency** | Messaging varies across channels. | Brand Guidelines, centralised oversight. |
| **Misinformation** | False or misleading information. | Fact-checking, official channels. |
| **Reputational Damage** | Negative publicity or association. | Transparent communication, due diligence. |
| **Unauthorised Use of Branding** | Fake accounts and websites. | Official verification, reporting. |

Mitigation measures include:
- Editorial review (Content Strategy);
- Brand Guidelines (consistent messaging);
- Compliance review (legal and regulatory);
- Version-controlled documentation;
- Official communication channels.
