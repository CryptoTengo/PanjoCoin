# 1. Overview

This document describes the liquidity allocation, liquidity locking strategy, and transparency framework of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

Liquidity locking is implemented to increase community confidence, improve transparency, and reduce risks associated with unauthorized liquidity removal.

The purpose of the liquidity lock is to ensure that the initial liquidity provided for PNJC trading remains secured during the defined lock period.
