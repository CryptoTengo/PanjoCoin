##1. Overview
This document defines the Vision and Mission of CryptoTengo and the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

The Vision describes the long‑term future that PNJC aims to create.

The Mission defines the practical actions and principles used to achieve this vision.

## 2. Vision Statement
Our Vision
To build a trusted global blockchain ecosystem where digital assets, community participation, decentralised governance, and real‑world social impact create sustainable value for users worldwide.

PNJC envisions a world where Web3 technology empowers communities, rewards positive contributions, and creates measurable social impact — inspired by the story of Panjo, a Shar Pei who brought hope to a children's hospital in Tbilisi through small acts of kindness.

## 4. Mission Statement
Our Mission
To develop a transparent, sustainable, and community‑driven blockchain ecosystem that combines SocialFi, GameFi, and Impact Economy principles to create real utility and positive social value — without relying on speculation or artificial mechanisms.

## 5. Mission Objectives
CryptoTengo focuses on:

5.1. Build Meaningful Web3 Utility
Create applications and services where PNJC provides real ecosystem value.

Objectives:

Develop utility functions (gaming, charity, governance);

Encourage participation through rewards and recognition;

Support ecosystem growth and sustainability.

5.2. Create SocialFi Innovation
Develop systems where social contribution becomes measurable and verifiable.

Key technologies:

Proof of Kindness (PoK) – recognising positive community actions;

Impact Score – measuring contribution value and reputation;

DAO Governance – decentralised decision‑making (Q4 2026).

5.3. Connect Blockchain With Real‑World Impact
Use blockchain transparency for:

Charity programs with on‑chain reporting;

Community initiatives and grants;

Social contribution tracking and verification.

5.4. Build a Sustainable Economy
Develop:

Responsible token economics (fixed supply, zero tax);

Long‑term ecosystem incentives (vesting, liquidity lock);

Transparent treasury management (multisig + DAO).

5.5. Empower Communities
Create opportunities for:

Users and holders;

Creators and contributors;

Developers and builders;

Partners and ambassadors.

to participate in ecosystem development.
