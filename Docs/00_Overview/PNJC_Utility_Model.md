# 1. Overview

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is designed as a utility token powering the Panjo ecosystem — a SocialFi platform where positive human actions, community participation, and social impact activities can be recognized, measured, and rewarded.

Unlike traditional meme tokens that rely primarily on speculation and community sentiment, PNJC is designed to have functional utility within an ecosystem built around:

- Proof of Kindness (PoK)
- Impact Score reputation system
- Community rewards
- Digital achievements
- NFT ecosystem
- DAO governance
- Partner programs

The core principle:

> Positive actions create measurable impact.  
> Impact creates reputation.  
> Reputation creates rewards.

USER ACTION
↓
VERIFICATION & IMPACT SCORE
↓
IMPACT POINTS REWARD
↓
PNJC TOKEN UTILITY
↓
ECOSYSTEM PARTICIPATION


This creates a sustainable loop where users are encouraged to contribute value to the community.

# 3. Stage 1 — User Actions

## Proof of Kindness (PoK)

Users participate in verified positive actions, including:

- Helping people in need
- Supporting charitable initiatives
- Environmental activities
- Community assistance
- Educational contributions
- Social impact campaigns

Users submit evidence of participation through the Panjo ecosystem.

Possible verification methods:

- Community validation
- Partner confirmation
- AI-assisted verification
- Reputation-based verification

---

# 4. Stage 2 — Impact Score System

Each verified action contributes to the user's Impact Score.

Impact Score represents a user's positive contribution history inside the Panjo ecosystem.

Users can progress through reputation levels:

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Bronze Hero | New community participant |
| Silver Hero | Regular contributor |
| Gold Hero | Verified impact contributor |
| Diamond Hero | High-impact community member |
| Global Hero | Exceptional worldwide contribution |

Impact Score is designed as a reputation layer, not as a financial instrument.

---

# 5. Stage 3 — Impact Points Rewards

After successful verification, users receive Impact Points.

Impact Points are internal ecosystem points used to measure contribution.

Reward factors may include:

- Type of action
- Social impact level
- Community participation
- Campaign difficulty
- Verification confidence

Example:

User helps organize environmental activity

    ↓

Action verified

    ↓

# 9. Anti-Gaming Principles

To maintain ecosystem integrity:

PanjoCoin will prioritize:

- Verified actions;
- Reputation-based rewards;
- Community validation;
- Anti-farming mechanisms;
- Transparent reward rules.

The goal is not to reward activity alone, but to reward meaningful contribution.

# 10. Role of PNJC in the Panjo Ecosystem

PNJC is designed as:

✅ A utility token  
✅ A community participation mechanism  
✅ A reward instrument inside the ecosystem  
✅ A governance tool for future DAO development  
✅ A connector between users, partners, and social impact initiatives

PNJC does not represent ownership of the Panjo ecosystem and is not designed as a promise of financial return.
