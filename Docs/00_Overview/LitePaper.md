# 1. Introduction

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is an open-source utility token built on the Polygon blockchain with the objective of supporting a transparent, community-driven ecosystem that combines blockchain technology, artificial intelligence, education, and social engagement.

The project is designed with a long-term ecosystem perspective rather than focusing solely on speculative trading. PNJC aims to provide practical utility through future ecosystem services, community participation, and responsible innovation.
