# 1. Introduction

## A Community-Driven Web3 Ecosystem

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is a blockchain-based ecosystem built on Polygon PoS.

The project combines:

- Blockchain technology
- Community participation
- Digital entertainment
- Gaming
- Artificial Intelligence
- Social impact initiatives

PanjoCoin is designed as a utility-focused ecosystem token supporting future digital experiences and community-driven applications.

The ecosystem is built around **Panjo**, a digital Shar-Pei character representing:

- loyalty;
- friendship;
- compassion;
- protection;
- positive human connection.

---

# 2. Panjo Legend

## The Story Behind the Symbol

Every ecosystem needs a recognizable identity.

The story of Panjo begins with a simple idea:

> Small acts of kindness can create meaningful global change.

Panjo is a Shar-Pei companion who travels around the world discovering positive human actions.

During his journey, Panjo discovers:

- people helping communities;
- volunteers supporting others;
- animal protection initiatives;
- educational projects;
- acts of kindness without expectation of reward.

These experiences become the foundation of the Panjo digital universe.
