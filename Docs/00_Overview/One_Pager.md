## Overview
PanjoCoin (PNJC) is an open‑source utility token built on the Polygon PoS blockchain to support a transparent, community‑driven ecosystem focused on blockchain technology, gaming, charitable initiatives, and decentralised governance.

The project emphasises long‑term ecosystem development through practical utility, responsible governance, open‑source development, and community participation rather than short‑term speculation.

Inspired by the story of Panjo — a Shar Pei who brought hope to a children's hospital in Tbilisi — the project embodies the principle that even a small act of kindness can change someone's reality.

## Vision
To build a trusted global blockchain ecosystem where digital assets, community participation, decentralised governance, and real‑world social impact create sustainable value for users worldwide.

## Mission
- Develop practical blockchain utilities;
- Build a gaming ecosystem (ONE+) with real utility;
- Support charitable initiatives with on‑chain transparency;
- Encourage community participation through DAO governance;
- Foster transparency through open‑source development, including honest disclosure of what is and isn't deployed yet;
- Expand responsibly within applicable legal and regulatory frameworks.
