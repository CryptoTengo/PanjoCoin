## 1. Project Overview
Project Name: PanjoCoin
Symbol: PNJC
Creator / Brand: CryptoTengo

Official Concept:

PanjoCoin is a blockchain-based ecosystem combining:

SocialFi – community engagement and digital reputation;

GameFi – gaming platform (ONE+) with in‑game economy;

Transparent Charity – on‑chain charitable contributions;

DAO Governance – decentralised community decision‑making;

Proof of Kindness (PoK) – recognition of positive community actions.

The project aims to demonstrate how blockchain technology can support positive social interaction, community participation, and real‑world impact — inspired by the story of Panjo, a Shar Pei who brought hope to a children's hospital in Tbilisi.

## 2. Vision

To build a trusted global blockchain ecosystem where digital assets, community participation, decentralised governance, and real‑world social impact create sustainable value for users worldwide.
