## 1. Overview

This document defines the **fundraising strategy** for the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

The purpose of this strategy is to establish a transparent framework for securing financial resources that support product development, infrastructure, security, community initiatives, and long-term ecosystem sustainability.

Fundraising activities should be conducted in accordance with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, and the project's governance principles.

### 1.1. Context

PanjoCoin is a Web3 ecosystem combining blockchain technology, SocialFi, GameFi, charitable initiatives, and transparent governance. The project is built on **Polygon PoS** with a fixed-supply, ownerless utility token.

## 2. Strategic Objectives

The fundraising strategy aims to:

| Objective | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Support Ecosystem Development** | Fund the development of core products and services. |
| **Finance Technology and Infrastructure** | Build and maintain secure, scalable blockchain infrastructure. |
| **Strengthen Cybersecurity** | Fund independent audits, testing, and security monitoring. |
| **Expand Educational Initiatives** | Develop educational content and community learning programs. |
| **Grow the Global Community** | Support community building and user acquisition. |
| **Build Strategic Partnerships** | Establish collaborations with Web3 companies, NGOs, and institutions. |

## 3. Guiding Principles

Fundraising activities should be:

| Principle | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Transparent** | All significant funding activities and allocations should be publicly documented. |
| **Responsible** | Funds should be used for their intended purposes and aligned with project priorities. |
| **Legally Compliant** | All activities should comply with applicable laws and regulations. |
| **Ethical** | Funding sources should align with the project's values and mission. |
| **Well Documented** | Clear records of funding sources, use of funds, and governance approvals. |
| **Consistent with Mission** | All fundraising should support the project's vision and long-term sustainability. |

**The project does not promote fundraising through misleading claims or guarantees of financial returns.**

## 5. Funding Sources

### 5.1. Grants

**Examples:**
- Technology innovation grants;
- Blockchain ecosystem grants (Polygon, Ethereum Foundation);
- Research grants;
- Educational grants;
- Social impact grants.

**Strategy:**
- Identify grant opportunities aligned with PNJC's mission.
- Prepare transparent grant proposals.
- Report on grant outcomes and impact.

### 5.2. Strategic Partnerships

Funding may be received through:
- Joint development projects;
- Research collaborations;
- Technology partnerships;
- Ecosystem integration programs.

**Strategy:**
- Build relationships with aligned organisations.
- Develop mutually beneficial partnership agreements.
- Ensure partnerships support ecosystem growth.

### 5.3. Corporate Sponsorships

Potential sponsors may support:
- Educational events;
- Community initiatives;
- Technical workshops;
- Open-source development.

**Strategy:**
- Identify sponsors aligned with PNJC's values.
- Develop sponsorship packages with clear deliverables.
- Ensure sponsorships do not compromise project independence.

### 5.4. Donations

The project may accept voluntary donations that support:
- Open-source development;
- Educational content;
- Community programs;
- Social impact initiatives (Charity Reserve).

**Important:** Acceptance of donations does not create governance rights unless explicitly defined.

### 5.5. Equity or Project-Level Investment

Where appropriate and legally structured, funding may be sought through investment in the operating entity or specific project initiatives rather than through promises relating to token value.

**Important:** Any such fundraising should be conducted under applicable corporate and securities laws.

## 6. Fundraising Lifecycle

Every significant funding opportunity should follow:

```
Opportunity Identification
        ↓
Evaluation (Strategic Alignment, Legal Review)
        ↓
Due Diligence
        ↓
Proposal Preparation
        ↓
Negotiation
        ↓
Agreement
        ↓
Fund Allocation (Treasury/Governance Approval)
        ↓
Reporting (Transparency Framework)
        ↓
Impact Review
```

---

## 7. Evaluation Criteria

Potential funding opportunities should be assessed based on:

| Criteria | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Strategic Alignment** | Does the funding support PNJC's vision and mission? |
| **Legal Considerations** | Is the funding source and structure legally compliant? |
| **Financial Sustainability** | Does the funding support long-term ecosystem health? |
| **Reputational Impact** | Does the funding source align with PNJC's values? |
| **Community Benefit** | Does the funding benefit the community? |
| **Operational Feasibility** | Can the funding be effectively deployed? |

Funding opportunities that conflict with the project's values should not be pursued.

---

## 8. Due Diligence

Before accepting significant funding, reasonable due diligence may include:

| Activity | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Identity Verification** | Confirm the identity of the funding source. |
| **Organisational Review** | Assess the organisation's legitimacy and reputation. |
| **Compliance Assessment** | Review AML/CFT and sanctions compliance. |
| **Conflict of Interest Review** | Identify any potential conflicts of interest. |
| **Reputation Assessment** | Evaluate the funding source's public reputation. |
| **Source-of-Funds Review** | Verify the legitimacy of the funds (where appropriate). |

The depth of due diligence should be proportionate to the funding arrangement.

## 13. Risk Management

### 13.1. Identified Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regulatory Changes** | Evolving crypto regulations. | Legal monitoring, compliance framework. |
| **Funding Concentration** | Dependence on a single funding source. | Diversified funding strategy. |
| **Operational Delays** | Delays in product development. | Phased roadmap, milestone tracking. |
| **Budget Overruns** | Unexpected expenses. | Budget planning, controlled spending. |
| **Reputational Impact** | Negative association with funding sources. | Due diligence, ethical standards. |
| **Market Conditions** | Crypto market volatility. | Long-term strategy, multiple revenue streams. |

### 13.2. Mitigation Measures

- Diversified funding sources;
- Financial planning (Financial Model);
- Treasury controls (multisig);
- Regular financial reviews (quarterly);
- Governance oversight (multisig + DAO).
