# 1. Overview

This document defines the Charity Policy of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

The purpose of this policy is to establish a transparent, accountable, and sustainable framework for managing charitable activities within the PNJC ecosystem.

The Charity Policy governs:

- Charity Reserve management
- Donation processes
- Impact verification
- Community participation
- Transparency reporting
- Social impact initiatives


PNJC charity activities are based on:


Transparency

Verified Impact

Blockchain Accountability
