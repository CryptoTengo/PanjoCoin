## 1. Overview

This document defines the **Charity Policy** of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

The purpose of this policy is to establish a transparent, accountable, and sustainable framework for managing charitable activities within the PNJC ecosystem.

The Charity Policy governs:

- Charity Reserve management;
- Donation processes;
- Impact verification;
- Community participation;
- Transparency reporting;
- Social impact initiatives.

PNJC charity activities are based on:

| Principle | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Transparency** | All transactions publicly verifiable on-chain. |
| **Verified Impact** | Charitable initiatives must be verified and measurable. |
| **Community Participation** | Community input and oversight. |
| **Blockchain Accountability** | Immutable, transparent record-keeping. |

---

## 2. Charity Mission

The mission of PNJC Charity initiatives is:

> To use blockchain technology as a tool for creating measurable positive social impact.

The ecosystem supports initiatives focused on:

- Children and healthcare programs;
- Humanitarian initiatives;
- Education;
- Community support;
- Social development;
- Verified charitable causes.
