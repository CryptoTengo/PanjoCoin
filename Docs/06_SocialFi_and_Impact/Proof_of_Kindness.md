## 1. Overview

This document defines the **Proof of Kindness (PoK)** framework of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

Proof of Kindness is a SocialFi mechanism designed to recognise, verify, and measure positive human actions through blockchain technology.

The purpose of PoK is to create a digital system where:

- Positive actions can be verified;
- Social contributions can be measured;
- Community members can build reputation;
- Real-world impact can be recorded.

PoK introduces a new concept:

```
Proof of Work
        ↓
Proof of Stake
        ↓
Proof of Kindness
```

Where blockchain technology is used not only to transfer value, but also to recognise positive social contribution.

**Status:** 🔄 Conceptual (not yet deployed)
