## 1. Introduction

This **Regulatory Compliance Framework** ("Framework") defines the compliance principles, governance standards, and regulatory considerations applied to the PanjoCoin (PNJC) ecosystem.

PanjoCoin is a community-driven utility token operating on the **Polygon PoS** blockchain, designed to support:

- Digital community participation;
- Social impact initiatives (Charity Reserve, ClownCare Bridge);
- Web3 ecosystem development;
- Future utility applications (ONE+ Gaming Platform, DAO Governance).

This Framework establishes a foundation for responsible development and international regulatory readiness.

| Area | Description |

### 4.3. United States Regulatory Considerations

PNJC considers regulatory approaches used in the United States, including analysis based on:

- Digital asset classification;
- Securities law principles (Howey Test);
- Consumer protection requirements.

The project evaluates factors commonly considered under the **SEC Howey Test**, including:

| Factor | Analysis |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Investment of Money** | PNJC is not marketed as an investment. |
| **Common Enterprise** | No common pool for profit generation. |
| **Expectation of Profit** | No promises of profit or dividends. |
| **Reliance on Managerial Efforts** | Token holders do not rely on developer efforts for profit. |

**PNJC does not represent:**
- Equity ownership;
- Company shares;
- Debt instruments;
- Profit guarantees.

**Refer to:** Legal Disclaimer (Howey Test analysis).

## 9. Governance Compliance

PNJC aims to establish transparent governance through:

### 9.1. Treasury Governance

- **3-of-5 Multisig** for Operational Treasury;
- Transaction approval procedures;
- Public reporting and on-chain verification;
- DAO Treasury controlled by community (Q4 2026).

### 9.2. DAO Governance (Future)

Future DAO mechanisms may include:

- Community proposals;
- Voting procedures (1 PNJC = 1 vote);
- Treasury decisions;
- Ecosystem development decisions;
- Charity program approvals.

**Parameters:**
- Quorum: 4% of total supply;
- Voting Period: 7 days;
- Proposal Execution: 48-hour timelock.

**Refer to:** Treasury Policy, Treasury Management.
