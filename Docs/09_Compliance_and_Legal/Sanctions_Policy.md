# PanjoCoin (PNJC)

# Sanctions Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** July 2026
**Project:** PanjoCoin (PNJC)
**Blockchain:** Polygon PoS
**Token Standard:** ERC-20

---

# 1. Introduction

This Sanctions Policy establishes the principles and procedures adopted by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to support responsible participation in the global digital asset ecosystem and to reduce risks associated with prohibited activities, sanctioned entities, and restricted jurisdictions.

PanjoCoin is committed to operating in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and international standards related to sanctions compliance, anti-money laundering (AML), and responsible blockchain development.

This policy reflects the project's commitment to transparency, security, and ethical ecosystem growth.

---

# 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

* Prevent the use of the PNJC ecosystem for prohibited activities.
* Reduce exposure to sanctioned individuals, organizations, and jurisdictions.
* Support compliance with applicable sanctions frameworks.
* Establish responsible operational principles.
* Protect users, partners, and ecosystem participants.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

* PanjoCoin project operations.
* Official communication channels.
* Ecosystem partnerships.
* Treasury management activities.
* Community programs.
* Future platform services.
* Business relationships.

This policy applies proportionally based on the nature of each activity.

---

# 4. Regulatory Framework

PanjoCoin considers relevant international sanctions frameworks, including where applicable:

## United States

* Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions programs.
* U.S. Department of the Treasury guidance.

## European Union

* European Union restrictive measures.
* EU sanctions regulations.

## United Kingdom

* UK sanctions regimes administered through relevant authorities.

## International Standards

* Financial Action Task Force (FATF) recommendations.
* International AML and Counter-Terrorist Financing principles.

## Georgia

PanjoCoin recognizes and intends to comply with applicable Georgian legislation regarding:

* financial restrictions;
* sanctions obligations;
* AML requirements;
* prohibited activities.

---

# 5. Sanctions Compliance Principles

PanjoCoin follows these principles:

## 5.1 Risk-Based Approach

Sanctions compliance measures are applied according to:

* operational risk;
* jurisdictional risk;
* partner risk;
* transaction risk;
* service risk.

---

## 5.2 Prohibited Activities

PanjoCoin does not support or intentionally facilitate:

* sanctioned activities;
* terrorist financing;
* money laundering;
* fraud;
* illegal financial operations;
* evasion of international sanctions;
* activities prohibited by applicable laws.

---

# 6. Restricted Participants

PanjoCoin may restrict interaction with:

* individuals listed on recognized sanctions lists;
* sanctioned organizations;
* entities controlled by sanctioned persons;
* prohibited jurisdictions where required by law.

Relevant sanctions lists may include:

* OFAC SDN List;
* EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions List;
* UK Sanctions List;
* applicable national sanctions lists.

---

# 7. Geographic Restrictions

PanjoCoin recognizes that certain jurisdictions may have restrictions related to:

* digital assets;
* cryptocurrency activities;
* financial services;
* blockchain-related activities.

Where legally required, access to certain services, campaigns, or partnerships may be restricted.

The project does not intentionally target users from jurisdictions where participation would violate applicable laws.

---

# 8. Wallet and Blockchain Considerations

PanjoCoin operates on a public blockchain environment.

Blockchain addresses are publicly visible and may interact with decentralized protocols beyond the direct control of the project.

The project recognizes that:

* blockchain transactions are transparent;
* wallet ownership may not always be identifiable;
* decentralized environments require risk-based monitoring approaches.

Where applicable and technically feasible, the project may use available compliance tools and industry practices.

---

# 9. Treasury and Operational Controls

PanjoCoin treasury activities should follow responsible management principles, including:

* transparent allocation;
* controlled access;
* documented decisions;
* responsible counterparties;
* avoidance of sanctioned entities.

Future treasury operations may include additional compliance controls.

---

# 10. Partnerships and Third Parties

Before establishing significant partnerships, PanjoCoin may evaluate:

* business reputation;
* jurisdiction;
* regulatory status;
* sanctions exposure;
* compliance practices.

The project reserves the right to decline relationships presenting unacceptable compliance risks.

---

# 11. Community and Marketing Activities

Official community activities should not:

* promote illegal activities;
* encourage sanctions evasion;
* target restricted jurisdictions improperly;
* misrepresent regulatory status.

Community members are expected to follow applicable laws and platform rules.

---

# 12. Compliance Monitoring

PanjoCoin may implement appropriate measures including:

* policy reviews;
* partner screening;
* risk assessments;
* documentation procedures;
* compliance improvements.

The level of monitoring depends on the nature and scale of ecosystem activities.

# 13. Reporting Suspicious Activity

Participants who identify potential sanctions violations are encouraged to report concerns through official project communication channels.

Reports should include:

* description of the concern;
* relevant information;
* supporting evidence where available.

* restrict partnerships;
