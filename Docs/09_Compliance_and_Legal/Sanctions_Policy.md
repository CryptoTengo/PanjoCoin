## 1. Introduction

This **Sanctions Policy** establishes the principles and procedures adopted by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to support responsible participation in the global digital asset ecosystem and to reduce risks associated with prohibited activities, sanctioned entities, and restricted jurisdictions.

PanjoCoin is committed to operating in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and international standards related to sanctions compliance, anti-money laundering (AML), and responsible blockchain development.

This policy reflects the project's commitment to transparency, security, and ethical ecosystem growth.

---

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Prevent the use of the PNJC ecosystem for prohibited activities;
- Reduce exposure to sanctioned individuals, organisations, and jurisdictions;
- Support compliance with applicable sanctions frameworks;
- Establish responsible operational principles;
- Protect users, partners, and ecosystem participants.

---

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- PanjoCoin project operations;
- Official communication channels (X, Telegram);
- Ecosystem partnerships;
- Treasury management activities (multisig);
- Community programs;
- Future platform services (ONE+, ClownCare Bridge, DAO);
- Business relationships.

This policy applies proportionally based on the nature of each activity.

## 8. Wallet and Blockchain Considerations

PanjoCoin operates on a **public blockchain environment** (Polygon PoS).

**Key considerations:**

- Blockchain addresses are publicly visible;
- Wallet ownership may not always be identifiable;
- Decentralised environments require risk-based monitoring approaches;
- The PNJC token contract is **ownerless** (`owner = address(0)`) with **no administrative functions** to freeze or block addresses.

**The project recognises that:**

- Blockchain transactions are transparent;
- Decentralised protocols operate beyond the direct control of the project;
- Where applicable and technically feasible, the project may use available compliance tools and industry practices.
