## 4. Georgian Regulatory Environment

Georgia has developed a regulatory framework for virtual assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs).

### Key Regulatory Developments

| Development | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **VASP Framework** | Georgia has established a regulatory framework for virtual asset service providers. |
| **AML/CFT Compliance** | Georgia follows FATF recommendations for AML/CFT. |
| **Banking and Finance** | Cryptocurrency activities are subject to financial regulations. |
| **Taxation** | Cryptocurrency transactions may be subject to taxation. |
| **International Standards** | Georgia aligns with international digital asset standards. |

### Regulated Activities

The regulatory environment focuses primarily on businesses performing activities such as:

- Exchange between virtual assets;
- Exchange between virtual assets and fiat currency;
- Transfer of virtual assets;
- Custody of virtual assets;
- Operation of virtual asset trading platforms.

Regulated activities may require registration and compliance obligations.
