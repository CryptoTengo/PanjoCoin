## 1. Introduction

This **Intellectual Property Policy** defines the ownership, protection, licensing principles, and permitted use of intellectual property associated with PanjoCoin (PNJC).

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Protect the PanjoCoin brand and ecosystem assets;
- Define ownership of project materials;
- Establish rules for community and third-party usage;
- Support transparent collaboration;
- Protect contributors and ecosystem partners.

This document applies to intellectual property created, maintained, or used within the PanjoCoin ecosystem.
