# 1. Purpose of This Document

This document describes the intended utility, functionality, distribution structure, ecosystem role, and regulatory positioning of **PanjoCoin (PNJC)** under applicable U.S. federal securities laws.

The purpose of this document is to provide a structured factual framework for legal review by qualified U.S. securities and digital-asset counsel.

This document is **not** a legal opinion, SEC registration statement, SEC approval, no-action letter, or certification issued by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”).

Any final determination regarding the legal classification of PNJC must be made based on the applicable law, facts, circumstances, and an independent legal analysis by qualified counsel.

# 2. Executive Summary

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is designed as a blockchain-based utility asset intended to facilitate participation in the PanjoCoin ecosystem.

PNJC is intended to provide functional utility within an ecosystem incorporating:

* Proof of Kindness (PoK);
* Impact Score;
* Social Mining;
* Kindness Map;
* Kindness Challenges;
* NFT functionality;
* GameFi;
* ecosystem events;
* community participation;
* DAO-related functionality;
* partner programs; and
* other ecosystem utilities.

PNJC is not intended to represent:

* equity;
* ownership in a company;
* a share of profits;
* a claim against the issuer;
* a debt instrument;
* a guaranteed investment return; or
* an entitlement to dividends or distributions from business profits.

The project is intended to emphasize **use and participation rather than investment return**.

# 3. Regulatory Positioning

PanjoCoin is intended to function as a digital asset with practical utility within a blockchain ecosystem.

The project does not represent that PNJC has been approved, endorsed, registered, or qualified by the SEC.

1. whether PNJC constitutes a security;
2. whether the distribution or sale of PNJC constitutes an investment contract;
3. whether the intended utility of PNJC is sufficiently functional;
4. whether the manner of distribution creates an expectation of profit based primarily on the efforts of others;
5. whether restrictions should apply to U.S. persons or particular transactions; and
6. what disclosures and compliance measures should be implemented before U.S.-facing distribution or trading.

# 4. PNJC Functional Utility

PNJC is designed to serve as an ecosystem utility asset.

The intended utility model is:

**User Action**

↓

**Impact Points**

↓

**Impact Score**

↓

**PNJC Utility**

↓

**Ecosystem Participation**

Potential utility includes:

### 4.1 Ecosystem Access

PNJC may be used to access selected ecosystem features, services, activities, events, and digital experiences.

### 4.2 Proof of Kindness

Users may participate in the Proof of Kindness system by performing verifiable socially beneficial actions.

Examples include:

* helping other people;
* charitable activities;
* environmental activities;
* helping elderly persons;
* animal welfare;
* community activities; and
* other qualifying actions.

### 4.3 Impact Score

Impact Score is intended to represent a user's participation and contribution within the ecosystem.

The system may include achievement levels such as:

* Bronze Hero;
* Silver Hero;
* Gold Hero;
* Diamond Hero; and
* Global Hero.

Impact Score is designed as a reputation and participation mechanism rather than an investment metric.

### 4.4 Social Mining

Users may receive ecosystem recognition or rewards based on qualifying participation and social contribution.

### 4.5 Kindness Map

The ecosystem may provide a geographical visualization of qualifying community activities.

### 4.6 Kindness Challenges

Users may participate in structured challenges designed to encourage socially beneficial activities.

### 4.7 NFTs

PNJC may be used in connection with selected NFT-related ecosystem functions, including digital achievements, collectibles, memberships, or access utilities.

### 4.8 GameFi

PNJC may be integrated into PanjoCoin GameFi applications, subject to the rules of the relevant application.

### 4.9 Events

PNJC may provide access to selected ecosystem events, activities, or participation mechanisms.

### 4.10 Partner Programs

Third-party partners may elect to integrate PNJC into qualifying ecosystem services, campaigns, rewards, or participation programs.

# 17. Securities Law Analysis

Independent counsel should analyze PNJC under applicable U.S. federal securities laws, including the investment-contract framework.

The legal analysis should address:

### A. Investment of Money

Whether users are contributing value in connection with acquisition or participation.

### B. Common Enterprise

Whether the relevant structure creates a common enterprise under applicable jurisprudence.

### C. Expectation of Profit

Whether purchasers acquire PNJC with an expectation of profit.

### D. Reliance on Efforts of Others

Whether any expected profit is derived primarily from the entrepreneurial or managerial efforts of others.

### E. Functional Utility

Whether PNJC has meaningful functionality at the relevant time.

### F. Marketing and Communications

Whether promotional statements create investment expectations.

### G. Distribution Structure

Whether the method of initial distribution changes the regulatory analysis.

### H. Secondary Trading

Whether the manner in which PNJC is traded creates additional regulatory considerations.

# 27. Final Regulatory Position

PanjoCoin is designed as a functional ecosystem token intended to facilitate participation in blockchain-based applications and social-impact activities.

The project's intended model is based on:

**Utility → Participation → Ecosystem Activity**

rather than:

**Investment → Profit Expectation → Reliance on Promoter**

Nevertheless, the final legal characterization of PNJC cannot be determined solely by the project's description of its intended purpose.

A qualified U.S. attorney should independently evaluate the actual facts, distribution method, economic structure, marketing communications, functionality, and planned secondary trading before the project intentionally targets U.S. users.
