## 2. Howey Test — Preliminary Self‑Assessment of Token Status

Based on U.S. case law (*SEC v. W.J. Howey Co.*), the project has conducted its own internal analysis of the PNJC token to assess whether it meets the definition of an "investment contract." **This is a preliminary self‑assessment prepared by the project, not an opinion from a securities lawyer, and it has not been independently reviewed by qualified counsel in any jurisdiction. It should not be relied upon as a determination of PNJC's legal status.**

| Howey Test Criterion | Project's Analysis as Applied to PNJC | Project's Preliminary View |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Investment of Money** | Acquisition of PNJC is intended to be access to ecosystem services (gaming platform, charitable giving, governance participation) rather than an investment. | Does Not Appear to Meet |
| **In a Common Enterprise** | Proceeds from token transactions are not directed to a common pool for profit generation; tokenomics does not involve profit distribution among holders. | Does Not Appear to Meet |
| **With Expectation of Profit** | The Whitepaper and Tokenomics contain no promises of profit, dividends, or value appreciation; the token is designed to provide access to services, not to profit. | Does Not Appear to Meet |
| **Derived from the Efforts of Others** | The token is not designed to confer rights to project management (except for future DAO voting, once deployed, which does not confer property rights); holders are not intended to rely on developer efforts to generate profit. | Does Not Appear to Meet |

**PROJECT'S PRELIMINARY POSITION:** based on the analysis above, the project's own (non‑legal) view is that PNJC is not a security under the Howey framework. **This is not a legal conclusion, is not binding on any regulator, and has not been confirmed by qualified securities counsel.** Regulators are not bound by a project's self‑assessment, and this table should not be cited as proof of non‑security status.

## 5. Risk Disclosure

The full list of risks is provided in the **Risk Disclosure** document. Below are the main risk categories:

### 5.1. Market Risks

- Token price is market-determined and may fluctuate both upward and downward;
- Complete loss of token value is possible;
- The cryptocurrency market is highly volatile and susceptible to manipulation.

### 5.2. Liquidity Risks

- The ability to sell the token is not guaranteed;
- **No Uniswap V2 liquidity pool for PNJC exists as of this revision** — price discovery and the ability to trade PNJC on a DEX are not currently available;
- Once a pool is created, its depth may be insufficient for large transactions, particularly in the early stages of the project.

### 5.3. Technical Risks

- Possibility of unknown vulnerabilities in smart contracts. As of this revision, only an internal automated static analysis (Slither, 95/100, no critical/high findings) has been completed; independent professional audits (CertiK / Hacken) have **not yet started** for any contract, including the deployed token contract;
- Possibility of code errors leading to loss of funds;
- Risks associated with the Polygon network (outages, attacks, forks).

### 5.4. Regulatory Risks

- Cryptocurrency laws may change in any jurisdiction;
- Possibility of prohibitions or restrictions being introduced;
- Tax obligations may arise at any time;
- The project's own assessment of PNJC's non‑security status (Section 2) has not been confirmed by qualified legal counsel and could be challenged by a regulator.

### 5.5. Development Risks

- Planned features (DAO, gaming, charity, vesting, treasury, liquidity lock, airdrop) may be partially implemented, delayed, or not implemented at all;
- Some features may be cancelled or modified;
- As of this revision, only the token contract is deployed to mainnet — every other module (Liquidity Locker, VestingVault, TreasuryVault, MerkleAirdrop, DAO Governor) is code‑complete but not deployed. See the README's "Current Project Status" table for live status.

### 5.6. Governance and Custody Risks

- Key wallet management is intended to be through a 3/5 multisig, but as of this revision **only 1 of 5 signers has been appointed** (the founder). The multisig cannot reach its 3‑signature threshold, meaning effective control of treasury operations currently rests with a single key;
- Full decentralisation will only be achieved after DAO Governor deployment (code ready; not yet deployed; targeted Q4 2026, subject to delay);
- Because the token contract is ownerless and has no blacklist, freeze, or pause function, the project has **no technical ability** to reverse transfers, freeze suspicious wallets, or otherwise enforce AML measures on‑chain. Any AML Policy the project maintains applies only to project‑controlled off‑chain processes (e.g., treasury operations, relationships with exchange or listing partners) — it cannot and does not restrict peer‑to‑peer token transfers.

### 5.7. Risks Associated with DEX

- Once a liquidity pool exists, trading on decentralised exchanges (Uniswap V2) will carry additional risks;
- No protection against MEV attacks (frontrunning, sandwich attacks);
- Inability to cancel transactions;
- Risks associated with AMM pool operations (impermanent loss).
