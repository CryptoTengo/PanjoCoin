## 3. Overview of MiCA Regulation

The Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) establishes a European Union regulatory framework for crypto-assets.

MiCA introduces requirements related to:

| Area | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Crypto-Asset Issuers** | Obligations for issuers of crypto-assets. |
| **Crypto-Asset White Papers** | Disclosure requirements for crypto-assets. |
| **Consumer Protection** | Safeguards for retail investors. |
| **Market Integrity** | Prevention of market abuse. |
| **Crypto-Asset Service Providers (CASPs)** | Authorisation and operational requirements. |
| **Stablecoins** | Specific rules for ARTs and EMTs. |

---

## 4. Potential Classification of PNJC

MiCA generally distinguishes between:

1. **Crypto-assets** — General category.
2. **Asset-Referenced Tokens (ARTs)** — Tokens referencing one or more assets.
3. **Electronic Money Tokens (EMTs)** — Tokens pegged to a single official currency.
4. **Other Crypto-assets** — Tokens not falling into the above categories.

---

## 5. PNJC Classification Assessment

### 5.1. Asset-Referenced Token (ART)

PNJC does not appear to qualify as an ART because:

- It is not designed to maintain value by referencing assets;
- It is not backed by currencies or commodities;
- It does not provide redemption rights based on referenced assets.

**Conclusion:** ❌ Not an ART.

---

### 5.2. Electronic Money Token (EMT)

PNJC does not appear to qualify as an EMT because:

- It is not designed to represent electronic money;
- It is not pegged to a single official currency;
- It does not provide monetary redemption obligations.

**Conclusion:** ❌ Not an EMT.

---

### 5.3. Other Crypto-Asset

Based on current design, PNJC may fall within the category of **general crypto-assets** under MiCA.

The final classification depends on:

- Token functionality (utility, fixed supply, zero tax, ownerless);
- Legal analysis;
- Actual usage;
- Marketing statements.

**Conclusion:** ✅ Likely falls under general crypto-asset category.
