## 1. Introduction

This **Compliance Program** defines the principles, procedures, and governance framework adopted by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to support responsible operation within the global blockchain ecosystem.

PanjoCoin is committed to developing an open, transparent, and responsible digital asset ecosystem while considering applicable legal, regulatory, and industry standards.

This Compliance Program establishes a foundation for:

- Anti-Money Laundering (AML);
- Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF);
- Sanctions compliance;
- Risk management;
- Responsible partnerships;
- Data protection;
- Operational transparency.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this Compliance Program are:

- To promote lawful ecosystem participation;
- To reduce exposure to financial crime risks;
- To establish responsible operational practices;
- To support transparency and accountability;
- To prepare the project for future institutional cooperation;
- To align operational practices with recognised international compliance principles.
