## 2. Market Risks

### 2.1. Price Volatility

- The Token price is determined exclusively by market supply and demand.
- The cryptocurrency market is characterised by extreme volatility, in which the price may change by tens of percent within a few hours.
- There is no price stabilisation mechanism or guarantee of minimum value.

### 2.2. Complete Loss of Value

- The Token may lose all of its value entirely.
- Historically, many cryptocurrency projects have lost 90–99% of their value, and some have become completely worthless.
- It is possible that the Token may become completely illiquid and have no buyers.

### 2.3. Market Manipulation

- Cryptocurrency markets are susceptible to manipulation by large holders ("whales"), including pumping (artificial price inflation) and dumping (artificial price collapse).
- "Pump and dump" schemes, fraudulent schemes, and coordinated actions to influence price are common.
- There is no government regulation protecting retail investors from such manipulation.

### 2.4. No Guarantees of Growth

- The Project provides no guarantees of Token price appreciation, utility, or liquidity.
- All forecasts, charts, and models presented in the Project's documents are solely informational and theoretical in nature and do not constitute promises of future results.

## 3. Liquidity Risks

### 3.1. Insufficient Market Depth

- In the early stages of the Project, the liquidity pool depth on DEX (Uniswap V2) may be limited.
- Large transactions may significantly impact the Token price (slippage).
- It is possible that selling even a small volume of Tokens may lead to a significant price decline.

### 3.2. Inability to Sell

- The ability to sell the Token is not guaranteed.
- In the absence of buyers (zero demand), the holder may be unable to realise their tokens at any price.
- In extreme cases, a complete loss of liquidity may occur, making trading of the Token impossible.

### 3.3. Impermanent Loss (for Liquidity Providers)

- Users providing liquidity to Uniswap V2 pools are subject to impermanent loss — losses arising from changes in the relative price of assets in the pool.
- Impermanent loss may exceed fee income, resulting in net losses.

## 4. Technical Risks

### 4.1. Smart Contract Vulnerabilities

- Despite code verification on PolygonScan and planned independent audits, unknown vulnerabilities may exist in the smart contracts.
- Discovery of a critical vulnerability could lead to loss of holder funds, Token lock-up, or other adverse consequences.
- Smart contracts are deployed **without upgradeability** (non-upgradeable). This ensures security but means that fixing discovered vulnerabilities is impossible without creating a new contract.

**Contract Address:** `0x51ba27A6EB41D879B03ed28eD0A5d6a2982B0BcF`

### 4.2. Code Errors

- Errors in smart contract code may occur, leading to incorrect functioning of functions (transfer, burn, permit, etc.).
- Errors may lead to loss of funds, irreversible transactions, or other undesirable consequences.
- Blockchain transactions are **irreversible** — user error (e.g., sending to the wrong address) cannot be corrected.

### 4.3. Polygon PoS Network Risks

- The Token is deployed on the Polygon PoS network. Users are subject to risks associated with the operation of this network:
  - **Technical failures** — blockchain stoppage, transaction processing delays.
  - **Network attacks** — 51% attacks, DoS attacks, consensus-level exploits.
  - **Forks** — blockchain splits that may lead to duplicate assets or other complications.
- The Project does not control the Polygon network and bears no responsibility for its operation.

### 4.4. MEV Attacks (Maximum Extractable Value)

- Trading on DEX (Uniswap V2) is subject to MEV attacks, including:
  - **Front-running** — anticipating a user's transaction to profit.
  - **Sandwich attacks** — placing transactions before and after a user's transaction to manipulate price.
- These attacks may result in unfavourable trade execution and additional losses.

### 4.5. Wallet and Private Key Risks

- Responsibility for the security of private keys rests exclusively with the User.
- Loss of private keys, phishing, wallet compromise, or use of insecure devices may result in complete loss of funds.
- The Project has no ability to restore access to the Token or cancel a transaction.

## 7. Governance Risks

### 7.1. Management of Key Wallets

- Currently, the Treasury wallet (120 billion PNJC, 12%) is managed through a **3-of-5 multisig**.
- Despite the multisig, there is a certain degree of centralisation, as keys are controlled by a limited number of individuals (founder, technical and strategic advisors).
- These individuals have the ability to spend Treasury funds, which may affect the market.

### 7.2. Full Decentralisation Only in the Future

- Full decentralisation of the Project will only be achieved after **DAO launch** (code ready; deployment scheduled for **Q4 2026**).
- Prior to DAO launch, decisions on Project development are made by the founder and team.
- There are no guarantees that the DAO will be launched on time or will function as intended.

### 7.3. Token Concentration

- A significant portion of the Token (50%) is in the liquidity pool, 12% in the Treasury, 10% in the DAO Treasury, which may lead to relatively high supply concentration.
- Large holders may exert substantial influence on the Token price.

## 8. DEX-Related Risks

### 8.1. Features of Decentralised Exchanges

- Trading on DEX (Uniswap V2) differs from trading on centralised exchanges and carries additional risks:
  - **No investor protection** — DEXs have no insurance funds, compensation mechanisms, or regulatory oversight.
  - **Irreversible transactions** — any transaction sent to the blockchain is irreversible.
  - **No customer support** — in case of an error, the User cannot contact the exchange's support.

### 8.2. AMM Pool Risks

- Uniswap V2 uses an automated market maker (AMM) model.
- Liquidity providers are subject to **impermanent loss** risk (described in Section 3.3).
- During sharp price movements, the pool may become imbalanced, leading to unfavourable exchange conditions.

### 8.3. No Order Execution Guarantees

- On DEX, there is no guarantee of order execution at the specified price.
- Large transactions are subject to slippage, especially in low liquidity conditions.
- In extreme conditions (high volatility, low liquidity), the User may receive a significantly worse price than expected.

## 10. Tokenomics-Related Risks

### 10.1. Token Unlocks

- In accordance with the circulation model, **5% of the locked supply enters circulation annually**.
- Gradual token release mitigates price pressure but does not eliminate it entirely.
- Token unlocks may create additional supply and exert downward pressure on price.

### 10.2. Team Vesting

- Team tokens (10%) and founder tokens (5%) are subject to vesting:
  - **Team:** 6-month cliff + 12-month linear vesting.
  - **Founder:** 12-month cliff + 24-month linear vesting.
- Upon vesting completion, the team may sell tokens, which may affect the price.
- There are no obligations on the part of the team regarding the timing or volume of sales.

### 10.3. Treasury Usage

- Treasury funds (120 billion PNJC, 12%) may be used for operational expenses, listings, and marketing.
- Treasury expenditure may result in tokens entering the market, creating additional price pressure.

### 10.4. Liquidity Lock

- 50% of total supply (500 billion PNJC) is locked through the immutable **PNJCLiquidityLockerV2** contract for **12 months**.
- Upon unlock, these tokens may enter circulation, which may also affect the price.
