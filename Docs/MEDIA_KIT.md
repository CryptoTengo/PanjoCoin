# 1. About PanjoCoin

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is a community-driven Web3 project built around the idea that blockchain technology can support positive social interaction, education, creativity, and real-world impact.

Inspired by Panjo — a Shar-Pei dog mascot representing kindness, loyalty, and community spirit — PanjoCoin combines:

- Meme culture
- SocialFi
- Proof of Kindness (PoK)
- Community rewards
- AI-powered content creation
- Web3 education
- Digital collectibles

PanjoCoin aims to create an ecosystem where participation, creativity, and positive actions generate measurable value.
