# 1. Introduction

This Business Continuity Management Plan (BCMP) defines the framework used by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to maintain critical operations during unexpected disruptions.

Blockchain projects operate in a complex environment where operational continuity may be affected by:

* cybersecurity incidents;
* technical failures;
* loss of key contributors;
* regulatory changes;
* market disruptions;
* third-party service failures.

PanjoCoin recognizes business continuity as an essential element of long-term ecosystem sustainability.

---

# 2. Purpose

The objectives of this Business Continuity Plan are:

* Maintain critical ecosystem functions.
* Reduce operational disruption.
* Protect project assets and information.
* Support recovery after incidents.
* Improve organizational resilience.
* Increase confidence among users, partners, and investors.

# 6. Business Continuity Risk Categories

---

# 6.1 Key Person Dependency Risk

## Description

Early-stage projects may depend on limited contributors.

Potential impact:

* slower development;
* delayed decisions;
* knowledge loss.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* documentation;
* shared knowledge;
* process standardization;
* automation.

---

# 6.2 Technology Failure Risk

## Description

Technical systems may experience:

* outages;
* software failures;
* infrastructure issues.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* backups;
* monitoring;
* recovery procedures;
* alternative solutions.

---

# 6.3 Cybersecurity Disruption Risk

## Description

Cyber incidents may interrupt operations.

Examples:

* account compromise;
* infrastructure attacks;
* unauthorized access.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* security controls;
* access management;
* incident response procedures.

---

# 6.4 Regulatory Disruption Risk

## Description

Changes in regulation may affect operations.

Potential impact:

* additional compliance requirements;
* operational restrictions.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* regulatory monitoring;
* compliance documentation;
* legal assessment.

---

# 6.5 Market Crisis Risk

## Description

Extreme market conditions may affect ecosystem activity.

Potential impact:

* reduced liquidity;
* lower user activity;
* funding challenges.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* controlled expenses;
* long-term strategy;
* diversified development priorities.

---

# 6.6 Third-Party Dependency Risk

## Description

External providers may become unavailable.

Examples:

* hosting;
* analytics;
* communication platforms.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* alternative providers;
* data backups;
* dependency monitoring.

# 7. Business Continuity Strategy

PNJC continuity strategy includes:

---

## 7.1 Documentation-Based Continuity

Critical knowledge should be maintained through:

* GitHub documentation;
* technical specifications;
* operational procedures.

---

## 7.2 Decentralized Architecture

Blockchain infrastructure provides:

* distributed network operation;
* transparent transaction history;
* reduced dependence on centralized systems.

---

## 7.3 Secure Access Management

Controls:

* strong authentication;
* restricted permissions;
* secure backups.

---

## 7.4 Operational Prioritization

During disruptions, priority functions include:

1. Protect users.
2. Maintain blockchain accessibility.
3. Secure communication channels.
4. Restore critical services.
5. Resume development activities.

---

# 8. Recovery Objectives

## Recovery Time Objective (RTO)

Target time required to restore critical operations.

Examples:

* communication channels: priority restoration;
* website availability: timely recovery;
* documentation access: maintained through backups.

---

## Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

Maximum acceptable data loss.

Objectives:

* minimize loss of operational information;
* maintain updated documentation.

---

# 9. Backup Management
