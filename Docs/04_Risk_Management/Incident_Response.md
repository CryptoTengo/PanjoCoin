# 1. Introduction

This Incident Response Plan defines the procedures used by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to detect, assess, respond to, recover from, and learn from security, operational, technical, and reputational incidents.

Blockchain ecosystems operate in a constantly changing environment where incidents may affect:

* smart contracts;
* digital infrastructure;
* community channels;
* reputation;
* operational continuity;
* user confidence.

PanjoCoin is committed to a transparent, responsible, and structured incident response approach.

---

# 2. Purpose

The objectives of this Incident Response Plan are:

* Establish clear response procedures.
* Minimize incident impact.
* Protect ecosystem participants.
* Ensure timely communication.
* Restore normal operations.
* Improve future resilience.

# 5. Incident Severity Classification

## Critical

Definition:

An incident causing or potentially causing:

* major ecosystem disruption;
* smart contract compromise;
* significant user impact;
* severe reputational damage.

Response priority:

Immediate.

---

## High

Definition:

Serious incident requiring urgent action.

Examples:

* compromised account;
* major phishing campaign;
* important infrastructure failure.

Response priority:

Within hours.

---

## Medium

Definition:

Limited impact requiring corrective action.

Examples:

* documentation errors;
* minor security issues.

Response priority:

Within normal operational procedures.

---

## Low

Definition:

Minor issue with limited impact.

Examples:

* small technical problems;
* non-critical improvements.

# 7. Incident Response Lifecycle

The PNJC response process consists of:

1. Preparation.
2. Detection.
3. Assessment.
4. Containment.
5. Resolution.
6. Recovery.
7. Post-Incident Review.

---

# 8. Phase 1 — Preparation

Preparation activities include:

* maintaining documentation;
* securing access;
* defining responsibilities;
* maintaining backups;
* monitoring security developments.

---

# 9. Phase 2 — Detection

Potential detection sources:

* security researchers;
* community reports;
* monitoring systems;
* internal reviews;
* blockchain analytics.

All suspected incidents should be documented.

---

# 10. Phase 3 — Assessment

The team evaluates:

* incident type;
* severity;
* affected systems;
* potential impact;
* required response.

Assessment questions:

* What happened?
* When did it happen?
* Which systems are affected?
* Are users at risk?
* What immediate actions are required?

---

# 11. Phase 4 — Containment

Containment actions may include:

* restricting compromised access;
* securing accounts;
* stopping unsafe processes;
* publishing warnings;
* coordinating with relevant parties.

---

# 12. Phase 5 — Resolution

Resolution activities may include:

* applying fixes;
* restoring systems;
* updating security controls;
* improving procedures.

---

# 13. Phase 6 — Recovery

Recovery includes:

* confirming system security;
* monitoring for recurrence;
* restoring normal operations;
* communicating updates.

---

# 14. Phase 7 — Post-Incident Review

After each significant incident, PNJC should evaluate:

* root cause;
* response effectiveness;
* lessons learned;
* required improvements.

---

# 15. Smart Contract Incident Procedure

In case of smart contract concerns:

## Immediate Actions

* verify incident details;
* analyze blockchain activity;
* assess severity.

## Technical Response

Possible actions:

* investigate code behavior;
* coordinate with security specialists;
* publish verified information.

## Communication

Provide:

* factual updates;
* risk information;
* recommended user actions.

---

# 16. Cybersecurity Incident Procedure

For cybersecurity incidents:

Actions may include:

* securing accounts;
* removing unauthorized access;
* investigating activity;
* strengthening controls.

---

# 17. Social Media Incident Procedure

If official channels are compromised:

Actions:

1. Confirm compromise.
2. Notify community through alternative channels.
3. Secure accounts.
4. Publish verification notice.
5. Restore control.

---

# 18. Phishing and Scam Response

If fraudulent activity is detected:

PNJC may:

* publish warnings;
* identify unofficial sources;
* educate users;
* report malicious activity where appropriate.

---

## Transparent

---

Provide updates when meaningful information becomes available.
