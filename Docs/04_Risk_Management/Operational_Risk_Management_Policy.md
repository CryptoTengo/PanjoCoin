## 1. Introduction

This **Operational Risk Management Policy** defines the approach used by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to identify, assess, monitor, and reduce operational risks associated with the development and management of the ecosystem.

Operational risk represents the possibility of losses, disruptions, delays, or failures resulting from:

- Internal processes;
- Human factors;
- Organisational structure;
- Technology dependencies;
- External service providers;
- Communication failures.

PanjoCoin recognises operational resilience as a critical component of sustainable blockchain ecosystem development.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this policy are:

- Identify operational vulnerabilities;
- Establish risk mitigation procedures;
- Improve organisational resilience;
- Reduce dependency risks;
- Protect ecosystem continuity;
- Support institutional readiness.

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

## 6. Key Operational Risk Categories

### 6.1. Organisational Risk

**Description:** Risks caused by limited organisational structure, unclear responsibilities, or insufficient resources.

**Potential Issues:**
- Dependency on individual contributors;
- Unclear ownership;
- Delayed decisions.

**Mitigation:**
- Define responsibilities;
- Maintain documentation;
- Create standard procedures;
- Improve delegation.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.2. Key Person Dependency Risk

**Description:** Risk resulting from reliance on specific individuals.

**Potential Impacts:**
- Development delays;
- Knowledge loss;
- Operational interruption.

**Mitigation:**
- Documentation of processes (GitHub, public docs);
- Shared access management (multisig);
- Knowledge transfer;
- Automation.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.3. Process Management Risk

**Description:** Risks caused by missing or ineffective procedures.

**Examples:**
- Inconsistent workflows;
- Missed deadlines;
- Communication failures.

**Mitigation:**
- Create SOP documentation;
- Maintain project trackers;
- Establish review cycles.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.4. Development Operations Risk

**Description:** Risks related to software development processes.

**Examples:**
- Insufficient testing;
- Poor release management;
- Undocumented changes.

**Mitigation:**
- Version control (GitHub);
- Code review (Code Review Policy);
- Testing procedures (Hardhat, Chai);
- Release documentation.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.5. Documentation Risk

**Description:** Risks related to incomplete, outdated, or inconsistent documentation.

**Potential Impact:**
- Reduced investor confidence;
- Operational confusion;
- Compliance issues.

**Mitigation:**
- Document ownership;
- Version control (semantic versioning);
- Regular reviews (quarterly);
- Centralised repository (GitHub).

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.6. Communication Risk

**Description:** Risks caused by inaccurate or delayed communication.

**Examples:**
- Inconsistent announcements;
- Misinformation;
- Unclear updates.

**Mitigation:**
- Official communication channels (X, Telegram);
- Approval procedures;
- Communication guidelines.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.7. Community Management Risk

**Description:** Risks related to community interaction.

**Examples:**
- Spam;
- Misinformation;
- Conflicts;
- Impersonation.

**Mitigation:**
- Moderation rules;
- Verified channels;
- Community guidelines (Code of Conduct).

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.8. Treasury Operations Risk

**Description:** Risks related to management of project-controlled assets.

**Potential Issues:**
- Unauthorised access;
- Poor allocation decisions;
- Insufficient controls.

**Mitigation:**
- Multisignature controls (3/5 approval);
- Transparent reporting (on-chain);
- Access restrictions;
- Documented approvals.

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (multisig controls)

---

### 6.9. Vendor and Third-Party Risk

**Description:** Risks caused by external dependencies.

**Examples:**
- Hosting providers;
- Analytics providers;
- Infrastructure services;
- Communication platforms.

**Mitigation:**
- Vendor evaluation;
- Alternative solutions;
- Service monitoring.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.10. Business Continuity Risk

**Description:** Risk of inability to maintain essential operations.

**Potential Causes:**
- Technical failures;
- Loss of access;
- Unexpected disruptions.

**Mitigation:**
- Backups (GitHub, database);
- Recovery procedures (Disaster Recovery Plan);
- Documented emergency processes (Incident Response Plan).

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.11. Roadmap Execution Risk

**Description:** Risk of failing to execute the development roadmap on schedule.

**Potential Impacts:**
- Delayed utility deployment (ONE+, ClownCare Bridge);
- Reduced community confidence;
- Missed market opportunities.

**Mitigation:**
- Milestone tracking;
- Phased development (24-month roadmap);
- Transparent reporting;
- Community communication.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

## 9. Incident Response

Operational incidents should follow:

### Step 1 — Detection

Identify and record the issue.

### Step 2 — Assessment

Determine:
- Severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low);
- Affected areas;
- Required response.

### Step 3 — Resolution

Implement corrective measures.

### Step 4 — Review

Document lessons learned (Incident Response Plan).
