# 1. Introduction

This Operational Risk Management Policy defines the approach used by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to identify, assess, monitor, and reduce operational risks associated with the development and management of the ecosystem.

Operational risk represents the possibility of losses, disruptions, delays, or failures resulting from:

* internal processes;
* human factors;
* organizational structure;
* technology dependencies;
* external service providers;
* communication failures.

PanjoCoin recognizes operational resilience as a critical component of sustainable blockchain ecosystem development.

# 6. Key Operational Risk Categories

---

# 6.1 Organizational Risk

## Description

Risks caused by limited organizational structure, unclear responsibilities, or insufficient resources.

Potential issues:

* dependency on individual contributors;
* unclear ownership;
* delayed decisions.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* define responsibilities;
* maintain documentation;
* create standard procedures;
* improve delegation.

---

# 6.2 Key Person Dependency Risk

## Description

Risk resulting from reliance on specific individuals.

Potential impacts:

* development delays;
* knowledge loss;
* operational interruption.

## Mitigation

Controls:

* documentation of processes;
* shared access management;
* knowledge transfer;
* automation.

---

# 6.3 Process Management Risk

## Description

Risks caused by missing or ineffective procedures.

Examples:

* inconsistent workflows;
* missed deadlines;
* communication failures.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* create SOP documentation;
* maintain project trackers;
* establish review cycles.

---

# 6.4 Development Operations Risk

## Description

Risks related to software development processes.

Examples:

* insufficient testing;
* poor release management;
* undocumented changes.

## Mitigation

Controls:

* version control;
* code review;
* testing procedures;
* release documentation.

---

# 6.5 Documentation Risk

## Description

Risks related to incomplete, outdated, or inconsistent documentation.

Potential impact:

* reduced investor confidence;
* operational confusion;
* compliance issues.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* document ownership;
* version control;
* regular reviews;
* centralized repository.

---

# 6.6 Communication Risk

## Description

Risks caused by inaccurate or delayed communication.

Examples:

* inconsistent announcements;
* misinformation;
* unclear updates.

## Mitigation

Controls:

* official communication channels;
* approval procedures;
* communication guidelines.

---

# 6.7 Community Management Risk

## Description

Risks related to community interaction.

Examples:

* spam;
* misinformation;
* conflicts;
* impersonation.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* moderation rules;
* verified channels;
* community guidelines.

---

# 6.8 Treasury Operations Risk

## Description

Risks related to management of project-controlled assets.

Potential issues:

* unauthorized access;
* poor allocation decisions;
* insufficient controls.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* multisignature controls;
* transparent reporting;
* access restrictions;
* documented approvals.

---

# 6.9 Vendor and Third-Party Risk

## Description

Risks caused by external dependencies.

Examples:

* hosting providers;
* analytics providers;
* infrastructure services;
* communication platforms.

## Mitigation

Controls:

* vendor evaluation;
* alternative solutions;
* service monitoring.

---

# 6.10 Business Continuity Risk

## Description

Risk of inability to maintain essential operations.

Potential causes:

* technical failures;
* loss of access;
* unexpected disruptions.

## Mitigation

Measures:

* backups;
* recovery procedures;
* documented emergency processes.
