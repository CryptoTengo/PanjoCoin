## 2. Important Notice Regarding Blockchain Data

PanjoCoin operates on public blockchain infrastructure (Polygon PoS).

**Blockchain transactions are:**

- Transparent;
- Publicly accessible;
- Permanently recorded;
- Generally irreversible.

When users interact with PNJC smart contracts (`0x51ba27A6EB41D879B03ed28eD0A5d6a2982B0BcF`), the following may become publicly visible:

- Wallet addresses;
- Transaction history;
- Token balances (PNJC);
- Blockchain activity (transfers, burns, approvals).

**PanjoCoin cannot delete or modify information stored directly on public blockchains.**

Users should understand that blockchain technology provides transparency by design. The ERC20Permit (EIP-2612) functionality enables gasless approvals, which also involve on-chain signatures that are publicly recorded.
