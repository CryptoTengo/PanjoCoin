## 1. Introduction

This **Financial Risk Management Policy** defines the principles and procedures used by PanjoCoin (PNJC) to identify, assess, manage, and mitigate financial risks associated with ecosystem development.

Blockchain projects operate in a highly dynamic environment where financial risks may arise from:

- Market volatility;
- Liquidity conditions;
- Treasury management;
- Funding limitations;
- Operational expenses;
- Token economics;
- External market factors.

PanjoCoin recognises responsible financial management as a key component of long-term ecosystem sustainability.

---

## 2. Purpose

The objectives of this policy are:

- Identify financial risks;
- Establish mitigation strategies;
- Improve treasury transparency;
- Support sustainable growth;
- Protect ecosystem stakeholders;
- Increase institutional confidence.

## 6. Financial Risk Categories

### 6.1. Market Volatility Risk

**Description:** Digital asset markets are highly volatile.

**Potential Causes:**
- Crypto market cycles;
- Investor sentiment;
- Macroeconomic conditions;
- Regulatory changes.

**Potential Impact:**
- Token price fluctuations;
- Reduced market confidence;
- Lower liquidity.

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on utility development (gaming, charity, governance);
- Transparent communication;
- Long-term ecosystem strategy (24-month roadmap);
- Responsible marketing.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.2. Liquidity Risk

**Description:** Liquidity risk represents the possibility that insufficient market liquidity affects trading conditions.

**Potential Impacts:**
- Higher price volatility;
- Reduced trading efficiency;
- Lower market confidence.

**Mitigation:**
- Liquidity planning (50% allocation);
- Transparent liquidity allocation;
- 12-month liquidity lock (PNJCLiquidityLockerV2);
- No emergency withdrawal.

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (liquidity locked for 12 months)

---

### 6.3. Treasury Management Risk

**Description:** Risks associated with management of project-controlled assets.

**Potential Issues:**
- Unauthorised access;
- Inefficient allocation;
- Excessive spending.

**Mitigation:**
- Multisignature wallets (3/5 approval);
- Access control (hardware wallets);
- Documented treasury procedures;
- Transparent reporting (on-chain verification).

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (multisig controls)

---

### 6.4. Funding Risk

**Description:** Risk that available financial resources are insufficient to achieve project objectives.

**Potential Causes:**
- Limited fundraising;
- Unexpected costs;
- Market downturn.

**Potential Impact:**
- Delayed roadmap execution;
- Slower ecosystem growth.

**Mitigation:**
- Controlled spending;
- Phased development (24-month roadmap);
- Multiple funding opportunities;
- Partnership development.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.5. Operational Expense Risk

**Description:** Risk caused by unexpected increases in operational costs.

**Examples:**
- Development costs;
- Infrastructure expenses;
- Legal costs;
- Marketing expenses.

**Mitigation:**
- Budget planning;
- Expense tracking;
- Prioritisation of critical activities;
- Multisig approval for significant expenses.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.6. Token Allocation Risk

**Description:** Risk related to improper perception or management of token distribution.

**Potential Concerns:**
- Concentration risk;
- Market pressure from unlocks;
- Insufficient transparency.

**Mitigation:**
- Public tokenomics;
- Vesting mechanisms (team: 6-month cliff + 12-month vesting; founder: 12-month cliff + 24-month vesting);
- Transparent allocation documentation.

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (vesting, public allocation)

---

### 6.7. Token Unlock and Vesting Risk

**Description:** Future token releases may affect market dynamics.

**Potential Impacts:**
- Increased selling pressure;
- Market uncertainty.

**Mitigation:**
- Predefined schedules (5% annual unlock);
- Transparent communication;
- Responsible release management;
- Long-term vesting alignment.

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (gradual release, transparency)

---

### 6.8. Dependency on Crypto Market Conditions

**Description:** The broader cryptocurrency market may influence liquidity, adoption, and investor interest.

**Mitigation:**
- Development independent from short-term speculation;
- Utility-focused ecosystem (gaming, charity, governance);
- Diversified growth strategy;
- Long-term vision.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.9. Exchange and Trading Risk

**Description:** Dependence on external trading platforms creates additional risks.

**Potential Issues:**
- Listing delays;
- Exchange policy changes;
- Liquidity changes.

**Mitigation:**
- Diversified ecosystem approach;
- Compliance preparation;
- Transparent communication;
- Uniswap V2 as primary DEX.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.10. Fraud and Financial Abuse Risk

**Description:** Potential misuse of the PNJC brand or ecosystem.

**Examples:**
- Fake investment offers;
- Fraudulent token sales;
- Impersonation.

**Mitigation:**
- Official communication channels;
- User education (phishing awareness);
- Verification mechanisms;
- Community moderation.

**Status:** 🔄 Ongoing

---

### 6.11. Charity Fund Mismanagement Risk

**Description:** Potential mismanagement of charity reserve funds.

**Potential Impact:**
- Reputation damage;
- Loss of trust.

**Mitigation:**
- Public Charity Reserve wallet;
- On-chain transparency (all transactions verifiable);
- DAO oversight (Q4 2026);
- ClownCare Bridge for transparent distribution (Q2 2027).

**Status:** ✅ Mitigated (public wallet, on-chain)

| Principle | Description |
| :--- | :--- |

| **Controlled Access** | 3/5 multisig approval for all transactions. |

| **Transparent Reporting** | On-chain verification of all movements. |

**Recommended Controls:**
- Multisignature wallets (Gnosis Safe);
- Transaction approvals (3 of 5 signers);
- Periodic reviews (quarterly).
