# Frontend Development Roadmap

Roadmap: PNJC Ecosystem Development
# Executive Summary
This roadmap delineates the strategic development milestones for the PNJC ecosystem. Our core focus is to ensure operational transparency, enhance platform utility, and maintain seamless interoperability with the Polygon blockchain.

# Phase 1: Foundation & Transparency (Q3 2026)
Objective: Establish project infrastructure and ensure public access to core documentation.

Website Deployment: Launch of the official project landing page.

Documentation Suite: Publication of the Whitepaper, Tokenomics, and Legal Disclaimer.

Contract Transparency: Public verification of the PNJC smart contract source code on Polygonscan.

Infrastructure Setup: Finalization of communication channels and repository structure.

# Phase 2: Analytics & Metrics (Q4 2026)
Objective: Empower ecosystem participants with real-time data and oversight.

Analytics Dashboard: Integration of live supply metrics and transaction volume monitoring.

Liquidity Tracking: Dedicated dashboard for monitoring PNJC/USDT trading pairs on Uniswap.

Holder Statistics: Transparent visualization of token distribution and circulating supply metrics.

Workflow Automation: Implementation of automated reporting and distribution workflows via n8n.

# Phase 3: Web3 Utility & Staking (Q1 2027)
Objective: Facilitate active user participation within the PNJC ecosystem.

Web3 Integration: Native support for MetaMask and WalletConnect for streamlined user interaction.

Staking Portal: Launch of the PNJC_Staking interface, enabling direct interaction with the staking smart contract.

Auxiliary Contract Deployment: Full deployment and verification of the Vesting, Multisig, and Liquidity Locker contracts.

# Phase 4: Governance & Social Impact (Q2 2027+)
Objective: Ensure long-term sustainability and social contribution.

Treasury Transparency: Real-time reporting on the allocation of project revenue to support the SmileDonate charity foundation.

Governance Portal: Implementation of community-driven voting mechanisms for ecosystem development proposals.

Ecosystem Expansion: Scaling community-led initiatives and optimizing automated growth workflows.

# Disclaimer: This roadmap represents our strategic vision for the PNJC ecosystem. Development priorities remain subject to adjustment based on community feedback, market dynamics, and technological advancements.
