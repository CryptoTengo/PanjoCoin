## Executive Summary

This roadmap delineates the strategic development milestones for the PNJC ecosystem. Our core focus is to ensure operational transparency, enhance platform utility, and maintain seamless interoperability with the **Polygon PoS** blockchain.

The roadmap is aligned with the project's 24-month development plan, as outlined in the Whitepaper, and prioritises:

- **Transparency** — Open documentation, public wallets, on-chain verification;
- **Utility** — Gaming (ONE+), charity (ClownCare Bridge), governance (DAO);
- **Security** — Ownerless contracts, zero tax, non-upgradeable;
- **Community** — DAO governance, community participation, incentives.
