# 1. Overview

This document defines the Market Making Policy of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

The purpose of this policy is to establish transparent principles for supporting:

- Healthy market liquidity

PNJC does not aim to artificially control token price.

The objective of market support activities is:

- Improve liquidity depth

- Improve trading experience

- Supply
- Demand
