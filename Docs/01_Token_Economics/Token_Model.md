## 1. Overview
PanjoCoin (PNJC) is a utility-focused ERC-20 token designed to power an ecosystem built around community participation, gaming, charitable initiatives, decentralised governance, and social impact incentives.

PNJC is designed not only as a transferable digital asset but as an ecosystem utility layer connecting:

Community participation;

DAO governance (Q4 2026);

GameFi experiences (ONE+, Q3 2027);

Transparent charity (ClownCare Bridge, Q2 2027);

Proof of Kindness (PoK) – conceptual;

Social Mining – conceptual;

Ecosystem services.
