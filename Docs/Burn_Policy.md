# 1. Overview

This document defines the Burn Policy of PanjoCoin (PNJC).

The purpose of the burn mechanism is to provide a transparent and controlled method for permanently reducing token supply.

The PNJC burn model is designed to support:

- Long-term scarcity
- Sustainable token economics
- Transparent supply reduction
- Community confidence


The burn mechanism follows:


Transparency

Automation

Permanent Supply Reduction

Responsible Token Economics


---

# 2. Burn Philosophy

PNJC uses a deflationary mechanism designed to gradually reduce circulating supply through blockchain-based burns.

The objective is not artificial scarcity, but:

- Aligning supply with ecosystem activity
- Supporting long-term economic balance
- Rewarding sustainable participation

# 6. Burn Process

The burn process:



Transaction Occurs

    ↓

Burn Percentage Calculated

    ↓

Tokens Sent To Burn Address

    ↓

Tokens Permanently Removed

    ↓

Blockchain Record Created

# 7. Burn Address Management

Burned tokens must be sent to:


0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD


or another publicly verifiable irreversible burn address.


Properties:

- No private key control
- No recovery possibility
- Permanent supply reduction
