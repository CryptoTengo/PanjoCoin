# PanjoCoin (PNJC) — Social Mining System

Version: 1.0  
Project: PanjoCoin (PNJC)  
Network: Polygon  
Category: Community Engagement / Web3 SocialFi  
Status: Planned Development  

---

# 1. Overview

PanjoCoin Social Mining is a decentralized community participation system designed to reward users for meaningful contributions to the PanjoCoin ecosystem.

Unlike traditional mining systems based on computational power, Social Mining rewards:

- Community growth
- Educational activities
- Content creation
- User engagement
- Ecosystem development
- Charity participation
- Knowledge sharing

The goal of Social Mining is to transform community members into active ecosystem contributors.

---

# 2. Vision

The PanjoCoin ecosystem is built around the principle:

> "Positive actions create measurable impact."

Social Mining connects human activity with blockchain rewards by creating a transparent mechanism where users can earn PNJC tokens through valuable contributions.

The system supports:

- Community-driven growth
- Decentralized marketing
- User-generated content
- Global kindness initiatives
- Web3 education
- Ecosystem expansion

---

# 3. Social Mining Principles

## 3.1 Contribution-Based Rewards

Rewards are distributed based on contribution quality, not only activity quantity.

The system evaluates:

- Authentic engagement
- Educational value
- Community impact
- Content quality
- Long-term participation

---

## 3.2 Anti-Spam Protection

PanjoCoin Social Mining includes mechanisms to prevent abuse:

- Bot detection
- Duplicate activity filtering
- Minimum quality requirements
- Reputation scoring
- Human verification mechanisms

---

# 4. Social Mining Activities

Users can earn Social Mining points through approved activities.

---

## 4.1 Content Creation

Examples:

- Educational crypto articles
- PanjoCoin ecosystem reviews
- Videos
- Tutorials
- Memes
- Animations
- Community stories

Reward factors:

| Activity | Impact Score |
|-|-|
| Educational article | High |
| Original video | Very High |
| Quality meme | Medium |
| Copy content | No reward |

- Participating in discussions
- Helping newcomers
- Answering community questions

Platforms:

- X (Twitter)
- Telegram
- Discord
- Reddit
- YouTube
- TikTok
- Instagram
- Facebook

---

# 4.3 Community Growth

Users can receive rewards for:

- Inviting active community members
- Organizing discussions
- Creating local communities
- Translating content
- Supporting regional expansion

Referral rewards are based on verified active users, not simple registrations.

---

# 4.4 Educational Contribution

Community members can earn points by creating:

- Crypto tutorials
- Blockchain explanations
- Web3 educational materials
- Security guides
- Research articles

---

# 4.5 Charity & Impact Activities

Social Mining integrates with PanjoCoin Proof of Kindness (PoK).

Users may receive rewards for:

- Charity participation
- Community assistance
- Verified positive actions
- Local impact initiatives
