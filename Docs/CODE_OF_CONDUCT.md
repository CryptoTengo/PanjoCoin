## 1. Our Pledge

We, the maintainers and contributors of PanjoCoin, pledge to make participation in our project and community a **harassment-free experience for everyone**, regardless of:

- Age;
- Body size;
- Disability (visible or invisible);
- Ethnicity;
- Gender identity and expression;
- Level of experience;
- Nationality;
- Personal appearance;
- Race;
- Religion;
- Sexual identity and orientation;
- Socio-economic status;
- Any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an **open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy** community.

---

## 2. Our Standards

### 2.1. Positive Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment:

- Using **welcoming and inclusive** language;
- Being **respectful** of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- Gracefully accepting **constructive criticism**;
- Focusing on **what is best for the community**;
- Showing **empathy** towards other community members;
- **Helping others** learn and grow;
- **Acknowledging** contributions and efforts of others;
- Acting with **integrity** and **transparency**.

### 2.2. Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- **Harassment** in any form – verbal, written, or physical;
- **Discrimination** based on any of the protected characteristics listed above;
- **Trolling**, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- **Public or private harassment**;
- Publishing others' private information (e.g., email addresses, wallet addresses) without explicit permission;
- **Spam**, unsolicited advertising, or promotional content;
- **Disruptive behaviour** in community channels (e.g., flooding, excessive off-topic messages);
- **Intimidation** or threats;
- **Inappropriate sexual attention** or advances;
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behaviours;
- **Misrepresenting** official project communications.

---

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces, including **GitHub repositories**, **issue trackers**, **pull requests**, and **discussions**;
- **Community channels** – official Telegram, X (Twitter), and any other communication platforms used by the project;
- **Public events** (online or offline) related to PanjoCoin;
- **Individual interactions** between community members when representing the project (e.g., at conferences, meetups).

This Code also applies when an individual is **officially representing the project** in public spaces. Representation includes using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

---

## 4. Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour. They have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct;
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviours they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful;
- Take any other action deemed necessary to maintain a safe and welcoming environment.

Maintainers are expected to act in a **fair, consistent, and transparent** manner, and to handle all reports confidentially and promptly.

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or have any other concerns, please **report it** as soon as possible.

### 5.1. How to Report

- **Email:** `conduct@cryptotengo.com` – this address is monitored by the project maintainers and is kept confidential.
- **Private Message:** You may contact any maintainer directly via private message on Telegram or GitHub (if you feel comfortable doing so). Please be aware that the maintainer will need to escalate the report to the team.

### 5.2. What to Include in Your Report

To help us handle the issue effectively, please provide:

- Your **contact information** (so we can reach you for follow-up);
- Names or usernames of the **individual(s) involved**;
- Description of the **incident** – what happened, when, where, and any witnesses;
- Any **supporting evidence** (screenshots, logs, links);
- Any **additional context** you think is relevant.

All reports are treated with **confidentiality** and will be investigated promptly.

---

## 6. Enforcement Process

When a report is received:

1. **Acknowledgement** – We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours;
2. **Investigation** – We will investigate the report, gathering relevant information while maintaining confidentiality;
3. **Decision** – Based on the investigation, we will determine the appropriate response;
4. **Action** – We may take one or more of the following actions:
   - A private warning to the offender;
   - A public warning;
   - Temporary or permanent ban from community channels or project participation;
   - Removal of contributions (comments, PRs, etc.);
   - Notification to relevant platform administrators (e.g., GitHub);
   - Escalation to law enforcement, if warranted.

The affected parties will be informed of the outcome, to the extent possible, while respecting the privacy of all involved.

---

## 7. Appeal Process

If you have been subject to an enforcement action and believe it was unfair, you may appeal. Appeals should be submitted via email to `conduct@cryptotengo.com` with the subject line **"Appeal"** and include:

- A description of the action taken;
- Why you believe it was unfair or disproportionate;
- Any supporting information.

The appeal will be reviewed by a different member of the maintainer team (if possible) or by an external third-party mediator. The outcome of the appeal will be communicated to you.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

All reports and related communications are treated as **confidential**. Information will be shared only with those who need to know in order to resolve the issue. We will not disclose your identity without your explicit consent, except when required by law.

---

## 9. Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies to all participants in the PanjoCoin project, including:

- Contributors (code, documentation, testing, etc.);
- Community members (users, token holders, event attendees);
- Maintainers and core team members;
- Partners and collaborators.

It also applies to **off-project interactions** where the individual's behaviour could reflect negatively on the PanjoCoin community or affect its members.
