# ❤️ PanjoCoin (PNJC) — Charity & Impact Initiative

## 🌍 Overview

PanjoCoin (PNJC) is a community-driven ERC-20 token that includes a long-term vision of supporting social and charitable initiatives through its ecosystem.

One of the key conceptual directions of the project is the **ClownCare Initiative**, focused on humanitarian and pediatric support programs.

---

## 🎯 Mission

The mission of the charity component of PanjoCoin is to explore how blockchain technology and community-driven funding can be used to:

- Support children's healthcare initiatives  
- Contribute to social and humanitarian programs  
- Increase transparency in donation-based ecosystems  
- Build trust through on-chain accountability (future goal)

---

## 🤡 ClownCare Initiative

The **ClownCare Initiative** is a charitable direction associated with the PanjoCoin ecosystem and the project founder’s philanthropic vision.

It is designed to support emotional, social, and medical assistance programs for children in healthcare environments.

## 🔗 Funding Model (Planned / Conceptual)

At this stage, PanjoCoin does not enforce automatic on-chain charity distribution.

Future potential mechanisms may include:

- Treasury-based allocation of ecosystem funds  
- Community governance decisions (DAO voting)  
- Transparent donation tracking systems  
- Smart contract-based allocation (future development)
