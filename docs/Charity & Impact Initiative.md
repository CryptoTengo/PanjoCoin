# ❤️ Charity Model — PanjoCoin (PNJC)

---

## STRUCTURE

- 5% of total supply allocated to Charity Wallet  
- allocation-based treasury model  
- no automated smart contract distribution  

---

## PURPOSE

Funds may support:
- humanitarian initiatives  
- medical support programs  
- pediatric care projects  

---

## TRANSPARENCY

- all transactions are visible on-chain  
- execution is off-chain (manual or multisig)  
- reporting is provided via documentation and receipts  

---

## IMPORTANT

This is not a protocol layer.
This is a treasury allocation model.
