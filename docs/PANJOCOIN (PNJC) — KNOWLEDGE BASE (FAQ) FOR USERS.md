2.8. What is slippage and how do I adjust it?
Slippage is the difference between the expected price and the actual execution price of a trade. Due to market volatility, the price may change between the time the transaction is created and executed.
• Recommended setting: 2-3%
• If transaction fails: Increase slippage to 5-7%
• Caution: Excessively high slippage may result in an unfavorable trade.

🔥 6. DEFLATIONARY MECHANISM (GOLDEN PHOENIX)
6.1. What is the Golden Phoenix Protocol?
The Golden Phoenix Protocol is an automated algorithm that burns (destroys) a portion of PNJC tokens, creating deflationary pressure and reducing supply, while also reserving funds in an insurance fund for price protection. The protocol is governed via multisig (3/5) and DAO.
________________________________________
6.2. How does the Golden Phoenix Protocol work?
The protocol executes the following steps in each cycle:
1.	USDT Inflow: The protocol receives USDT from external sources (partner fees, NFT marketplace revenues, voluntary contributions).
2.	Insurance Fund Reservation: 5% of incoming USDT is directed to the insurance fund to protect against sharp price declines (insurancePercent parameter, DAO-adjustable, range 3–10%).
3.	Buyback Amount Calculation (buybackPercent): From the remaining 95% of USDT, buybackPercent (default 25%) is allocated for token buybacks. The remaining funds are accumulated on the contract balance for future cycles.
Parameter adjustable by DAO within 15–35%.
Smart contract constraint: insurancePercent + buybackPercent == 30.
4.	Conversion: The specified amount is converted to PNJC via QuickSwap V2 through the path USDT → WMATIC → PNJC, respecting the maximum slippage (maxSlippageBps).
5.	Burning: 50% of purchased tokens are permanently burned (burnPercent parameter, DAO-adjustable, range 30–70%).
6.	Rewards: The remaining 50% are directed to the community rewards pool (PoK, Social Mining, Challenges) (rewardPercent parameter, DAO-adjustable, range 30–70%).
Smart contract constraint: burnPercent + rewardPercent == 100.
7.	Price Protection (optional): If the PNJC price falls below the established price floor, the multisig (4 of 5) may initiate the use of insurance fund funds for buybacks and burning to stabilize the price.
________________________________________
6.3. Why isn't buybackPercent set to 100%?
Technical rationale:
1.	Slippage Reduction: Large one-time buybacks can significantly increase the PNJC price, amplifying slippage. Gradual buybacks (25% per cycle) minimize market impact.
2.	Capital Accumulation: Accumulated funds allow for larger buybacks during periods of low liquidity, maximizing the deflationary effect.
3.	Management Flexibility: The DAO can adjust buybackPercent based on market conditions (increase in high liquidity, decrease in low liquidity).
4.	Strategic Reserving: A portion of funds remains on the protocol balance for emergency situations (e.g., activating price protection through the insurance fund without requiring external funds).
________________________________________
6.4. Why is burning beneficial?
• 📉 Supply Reduction: Fewer tokens create greater scarcity.
• 📈 Price Support: With constant demand, reduced supply creates upward price pressure.
• 🎁 Community Rewards: A portion of tokens is returned to active participants.
• 🛡️ Insurance Fund: 5% of USDT is reserved to stabilize the price if it falls below the established level (price floor).
________________________________________
6.5. How many tokens have been burned so far?
Burning statistics are updated in real-time and published in quarterly reports. You can track Golden Phoenix Protocol activity on PolygonScan via the contract address or through public dashboards.
________________________________________
6.6. Can the DAO change the burning parameters?
Yes! The DAO can vote to change:
• insurancePercent — insurance fund share (range 3–10%).
• buybackPercent — buyback share (range 15–35%).
Constraint: insurancePercent + buybackPercent == 30.
• burnPercent — burn percentage (range 30–70%).
• rewardPercent — rewards percentage (range 30–70%).
Constraint: burnPercent + rewardPercent == 100.
• priceFloor — price floor (subject to DAO review).
• minExecutionAmount — minimum amount for protocol execution (50–10,000 USDT).
• maxSlippageBps — maximum slippage (50–1000 bps).
